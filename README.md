# About Me

Personal notes on my career, motivations, and the things about myself I keep forgetting to write down.

## Why this exists

I noticed I keep forgetting my own reasons - the real ones, not the resume version. Things like why I actually mentor, or what I actually enjoy, surface in a good conversation or a hard interview and then vanish again until the next time I'm asked. This repo is where I catch them before they slip.

## What's in here

- `about-me.md` - the core file. My journey, why I mentor, things I'm still trying to remember about myself, and a bank of interview/application answers worth keeping.

## How I use it

Whenever something surfaces - in an interview, a conversation, a quiet moment - I add it here. Doesn't need to be polished, just captured. If a section outgrows the main file (interview answers are the likely candidate), it gets split into its own file at that point, not before.
